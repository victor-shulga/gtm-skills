# AI Writing Patterns — English

Based on [Wikipedia: Signs of AI Writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) (WikiProject AI Cleanup) and observed patterns across thousands of GPT-generated texts since 2023.

---

## CONTENT PATTERNS

### 1. Inflated significance, legacy, and scale
**Marker words:** stands as a testament to, marks a pivotal moment, represents a watershed, paradigm-shifting, transformational, indispensable role, cornerstone of, hallmark of, ushers in a new era, reshapes the landscape, redefines the boundaries, leaves an indelible mark, plays a crucial/pivotal/essential role, in the broader context of

**Problem:** AI inflates importance, attributing historical significance to arbitrary things.

**Before:**
> The Statistical Institute of Catalonia, established in 1989, marked a pivotal moment in the evolution of regional statistics in Spain. This initiative was part of a broader movement toward decentralization and the strengthening of regional autonomy.

**After:**
> The Statistical Institute of Catalonia was created in 1989 to collect and publish regional statistics separately from the national service.

---

### 2. Aggressive authority signaling
**Marker words:** widely regarded, leading experts agree, prominent figures have noted, garnered widespread acclaim, recognized authority, extensively covered in the media

**Before:**
> Her views have been cited in The New York Times, Forbes, and The Wall Street Journal. She maintains an active social media presence with over 500,000 followers.

**After:**
> In a 2024 New York Times interview, she argued that AI regulation should focus on outcomes rather than methods.

---

### 3. Shallow analysis via participles
**Marker words:** underscoring..., highlighting..., showcasing..., demonstrating..., reflecting..., embodying..., symbolizing..., shaping..., contributing to..., serving as a reminder...

**Problem:** AI tacks participial phrases onto sentences to fake depth.

**Before:**
> The temple's color palette of blues, greens, and golds harmonizes with the regional landscape, symbolizing the beauty of Texas plains, the waters of the Gulf, and the diversity of local nature, reflecting the community's deep connection to the land.

**After:**
> The temple uses blues, greens, and golds. The architect said the colors reference wildflowers and the Gulf.

---

### 4. Promotional and marketing language
**Marker words:** boasts, vibrant, bustling, picturesque, breathtaking, stunning, rich heritage, profound (figurative), unique, one-of-a-kind, truly, genuinely, in the heart of, unparalleled, it's worth noting, leaves a lasting impression, unlocks the potential, game-changer, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, world-class

**Before:**
> Nestled in the heart of the picturesque Gondar region of Ethiopia, the vibrant and bustling city of Alamata-Raya-Kobo boasts a rich cultural heritage and breathtaking natural beauty.

**After:**
> Alamata-Raya-Kobo is a city in Ethiopia's Gondar region, known for its weekly market and an 18th-century church.

---

### 5. Vague references and weasel words
**Marker words:** according to industry reports, observers note, experts believe, some critics argue, various sources suggest, available data indicates, studies have shown

**Before:**
> Owing to its unique characteristics, the Haolai River draws the attention of researchers and ecologists. Experts believe it plays a key role in the regional ecosystem.

**After:**
> The Haolai River hosts several endemic fish species, according to a 2019 Chinese Academy of Sciences study.

---

### 6. Boilerplate "challenges and prospects"
**Marker words:** despite facing numerous challenges, nonetheless continues to thrive, challenges and opportunities, in the face of adversity, despite these obstacles

**Before:**
> Despite its industrial growth, Koratur faces typical urban challenges — traffic congestion and water shortages. Nonetheless, owing to its strategic location and active initiatives, the district continues to thrive dynamically.

**After:**
> Traffic worsened after 2015, when three new IT parks opened. The municipality began building stormwater drains in 2022 due to recurring flooding.

---

## LINGUISTIC PATTERNS

### 7. AI vocabulary: words with inflated frequency
**Heavily over-used words (post-2023):** delve, dive deeper, navigate (abstract), tapestry, intricate, multifaceted, robust, leverage, harness, foster, underscore, elucidate, encompass, intersection (abstract), realm, landscape (abstract), pivotal, paramount, profound, seamless, comprehensive, holistic, nuanced, vibrant, resonate, align with, in line with

**Also:** it's worth noting, it is important to note, one must consider, it bears mentioning, importantly

**Problem:** These words appear in AI text far more frequently than in human writing.

**Before:**
> Furthermore, one of the distinctive features of Somali cuisine is the use of camel meat. The enduring legacy of Italian colonial influence is reflected in the widespread presence of pasta in the local culinary landscape, demonstrating how these dishes have seamlessly integrated into traditional diets.

**After:**
> Somali cooking uses camel meat as a delicacy. Italian colonizers brought pasta, which is still common in the south.

---

### 8. Avoiding plain "is/are"
**Marker phrases:** serves as, stands as, represents, embodies, exemplifies, constitutes, boasts

**Before:**
> Gallery 825 serves as the LAAA exhibition space for contemporary art. The gallery boasts four halls totaling over 250 square meters.

**After:**
> Gallery 825 is the LAAA exhibition space for contemporary art. It has four halls totaling 250 square meters.

---

### 9. Negative parallelisms
**Constructions:** "It's not just X, it's Y" / "This isn't only X, but Y" / "It's not about X, it's about Y"

**Before:**
> It's not just a beat under the vocals, it's part of the aggression and atmosphere. It's not just a song — it's a statement.

**After:**
> The heavy beat sets an aggressive tone.

---

### 10. Compulsive rule of three
**Problem:** AI groups ideas in threes for rhetorical force, even when only one or two points are warranted.

**Before:**
> The event offers keynote sessions, panel discussions, and networking opportunities. Attendees can expect innovation, inspiration, and industry insights.

**After:**
> The event has talks and panels, with time between sessions for informal conversation.

---

### 11. Excessive synonyms (elegant variation)
**Problem:** AI penalizes repetition internally and cycles through synonyms where repetition would be cleaner.

**Before:**
> The protagonist faces many trials. The hero must overcome obstacles. The central character ultimately prevails. The lead returns home.

**After:**
> The protagonist faces trials, ultimately prevails, and returns home.

---

### 12. False ranges
**Problem:** AI builds "from X to Y" structures where X and Y aren't on the same scale.

**Before:**
> Our journey through the universe took us from the singularity of the Big Bang to the grand cosmic web, from the birth and death of stars to the mysterious dance of dark matter.

**After:**
> The book covers the Big Bang, stellar formation, and current theories of dark matter.

---

## STYLE PATTERNS

### 13. Em dash overuse
**Problem:** AI uses em dashes far more often than human writers, mimicking an "energetic" style.

**Before:**
> This term is promoted by Dutch institutions — not by the people themselves. You wouldn't write "Netherlands, Europe" in an address — but this inaccuracy persists — even in official documents.

**After:**
> This term is promoted by Dutch institutions, not by the people themselves. You wouldn't write "Netherlands, Europe" in an address, but the inaccuracy persists in official documents.

---

### 14. Bold overuse
**Problem:** AI mechanically bolds every term.

**Before:**
> The methodology combines **OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)**, **KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)**, and visual tools: **Business Model Canvas (BMC)** and **Balanced Scorecard (BSC)**.

**After:**
> The methodology combines OKRs, KPIs, and visual tools like the Business Model Canvas and Balanced Scorecard.

---

### 15. Vertical lists with bold lead-ins
**Problem:** AI builds lists where every item starts with a bolded label and a colon.

**Before:**
> - **User Experience:** the user experience has been significantly improved through the new interface.
> - **Performance:** performance has been enhanced through optimized algorithms.
> - **Security:** security has been strengthened through end-to-end encryption.

**After:**
> The update improved the interface, sped up loading via algorithm optimization, and added end-to-end encryption.

---

### 16. Title Case in headings
**Problem:** AI defaults to Title Case even when sentence case fits the publication's style better.

**Before:**
> ## Strategic Negotiations And Global Partnerships

**After:**
> ## Strategic negotiations and global partnerships

---

### 17. Emoji in headings and lists
**Before:**
> 🚀 **Launch phase:** product ships in Q3
> 💡 **Key insight:** users prefer simplicity
> ✅ **Next steps:** schedule the follow-up

**After:**
> The product ships in Q3. Research showed users prefer simplicity. Next step: schedule the follow-up.

---

### 18. Smart quotes / curly quotes inconsistency
**Problem:** AI mixes straight and curly quotes inconsistently within the same document.

**Before:**
> He said "the project is on track," but the others "weren't sure".

**After:**
> He said "the project is on track," but the others weren't sure.

(Pick one quote style and apply consistently.)

---

## COMMUNICATIVE PATTERNS

### 19. Chatbot artifacts
**Marker words:** I hope this helps!, Certainly!, Absolutely!, You're absolutely right!, Let me know if you'd like..., Here's an overview..., I'd be happy to..., Feel free to ask...

**Before:**
> Here's an overview of the French Revolution. I hope this helps! Let me know if you'd like me to expand on any section.

**After:**
> The French Revolution began in 1789, triggered by financial crisis and food shortages.

---

### 20. Knowledge disclaimers
**Marker phrases:** as of my knowledge cutoff, to the best of my knowledge, specific data is limited, based on available information, the available sources do not contain

**Before:**
> While specific details about the company's founding aren't documented in widely available sources, based on available information, it was established sometime in the 1990s.

**After:**
> The company was founded in 1994, according to its registration documents.

---

### 21. Sycophantic tone
**Before:**
> Great question! You're absolutely right that this is a complex topic. Excellent point about economic factors.

**After:**
> The economic factors you mentioned are central here.

---

## WATER AND HEDGING

### 22. Filler phrases
**Before → After:**
- "In order to achieve this goal" → "To"
- "Due to the fact that it was raining" → "Because of the rain"
- "At the present moment in time" → "Now"
- "In the event that you need help" → "If you need help"
- "The system has the ability to process" → "The system can process"
- "It is important to note the fact that the data shows" → "The data shows"
- "Utilize" → "Use"
- "Leverage" (unless deliberately metaphorical) → use a plain verb
- "In terms of" (often empty) → cut or rephrase

---

### 23. Excessive hedging
**Before:**
> It could potentially be suggested that this policy might possibly have some degree of influence on the outcomes.

**After:**
> The policy probably affects outcomes.

---

### 24. Boilerplate positive endings
**Before:**
> The future of the company looks promising. Exciting times lie ahead as it continues its journey toward excellence. This is an important step in the right direction.

**After:**
> The company plans to open two new offices next year.

---

### 25. Bureaucratese / business-speak
**Marker words:** utilize, leverage, facilitate, operationalize, synergize, optimize (overused), in terms of, with respect to, in regard to, pursuant to, hereinafter, aforementioned, said (as adjective), going forward, at this time

**Before:**
> Pursuant to our discussion at the aforementioned meeting, we will operationalize the strategy and leverage existing capabilities to facilitate the optimization of outcomes.

**After:**
> Following our meeting, we'll roll out the strategy using what we already have to improve results.

---

### 26. Excess prefatory phrases
**Marker phrases:** It's worth noting that, It is important to emphasize that, One cannot overlook the fact that, It should be mentioned that, Equally important is, It bears repeating that

**Before:**
> It's worth noting that the company is growing. It is important to emphasize that revenue increased by 20%. One cannot overlook the team's expansion.

**After:**
> The company is growing: revenue is up 20% and the team has expanded.

---

### 27. Abstract "world / realm / landscape" framing
**Marker phrases:** in the world of, in the realm of, in the landscape of, in the space of, in the arena of, the intersection of (abstract)

**Before:**
> In the world of modern technology, artificial intelligence holds a special place in the realm of business process automation.

**After:**
> AI is increasingly used to automate business processes.
